Abstract

communication through  the use of natural language. Chat -bots converse 
with customers in a conversation based on human  input and provide 
customer service. Even though they are actually conversing with a

Message Interpretation 
Entity Identification. Topical keywords and geo-entities are two sorts of entities that our 
proof-of- concept prototype can identify. Entity mention extraction, the first  stage, is an  
example of the sequence labelling  task [4]. The text of the user's speech is provided as its 
input in the form of a  word list. After that, a supervised machine learning model called 
conditional random fields (CRF) is  trained to provide words labels. One of the topic 
keywords was present in 121 of the sample messages that were used to train the model, while 
one of the geo -entities was present in 18  examples. Following that, entity mentions are 
extracted using these labels. A text span that refers to  one of the entities, such as "schools" or

ALICE: 
Richard Wallace introduced ALICE in 1995. It makes use of pattern matching and stores the 
data in  files called AIML, or Artificial Intelligence Mark -up Language. An AIML file was 
created to contain pattern knowledge for chat-bots and is comparable to an XML file. Default 
categories, recursive categories, and atomic categories are the three different types  of AIML 
categories.

Results and Discussion 
The proposed system was tested and found to be workable and efficient. It conserves both the 
owners and employees' time and labour. Additionally, it saves staff members the time and 
effort of  making a special trip to the restaurant store to make a query. In this project, we 
created a chat -bot that would interact with users and p rovide all relevant information 
regarding food orders. The  owner and customer can communicate with a chat -bot. Any 
questions that are not answered by the chat-bot will be updated by the owner admin.
